Tanks are too powerful in the late game — you can stack dozens of them 
and survive any drought with zero effort. Expansion of deeper reservoirs and larger dams become 
optional. This mod fixes that.

Water tanks lose a configurable percentage of their maximum
capacity every in-game day. A Large Tank holding 1200 units loses 
60 per day at the default 5% rate. The loss is based on max capacity 
not current fill level — so a large tank always hurts more than a 
small one, making the early game forgiving while pressuring late game 
expansion toward reservoir infrastructure.

FEATURES
- Configurable evaporation rate: 1% to 20% (default 5%)
- Only water evaporates by default. Badwater, extract and other liquids build up much more slowly,
  so they're left alone unless you turn off the "Water Tanks Only" setting
- Tank inspect panel shows daily leak and days until empty
- Works on Small, Medium and Large tanks for both factions

1% rate — Large tank loses 12 units/day, empties in 100 days
5% rate (default) — Large tank loses 60 units/day, empties in 20 days
10% rate — Large tank loses 120 units/day, empties in 10 days
20% rate — Large tank loses 240 units/day, empties in 5 days

REQUIRED MODS
- Harmony
- Mod Settings

For players who want the dam infrastructure to matter!
